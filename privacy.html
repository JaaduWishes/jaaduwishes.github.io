Privacy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

What we collect

  • What you send us for an order: the occasion, names, your voice note or message, and any photos or videos.
  • Your WhatsApp number and messages, because orders happen on WhatsApp.

How we use it

Only to make and deliver your surprise, and to talk to you about your order. We don't sell your details or share your photos with anyone else. We only show a finished surprise as a sample on this site or on Instagram if you and the person it's for say yes.

Where it's stored

Your voice note and photos stay on your phone until you send them to us on WhatsApp; this website doesn't upload them anywhere. The surprise page we make for you is hosted at a private link that isn't listed on search engines.

Ads and analytics

We may use the Meta Pixel to measure how our Instagram and Facebook ads perform. It can set cookies and tells Meta when someone visits this site or taps to order. It does not receive your voice note, photos or message.

Deleting your data

Message us on WhatsApp (+91 73002 81228) and we'll delete your photos, voice note and surprise page.
